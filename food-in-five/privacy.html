Privacy Policy

Food in Five · Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Food in Five ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. Food in Five operates entirely on your device — there is no backend server, no user accounts, and no server-side data storage.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

  • Current Location: With your permission, the app accesses your current location (GPS coordinates) to find nearby restaurants.
  • Search Locations: When you search by address or ZIP code, the text you enter is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to convert it into coordinates (see Section 5.3). It is not stored on any server we operate.
  • Usage: Location data is sent directly from your device to Google Places API to retrieve restaurant results. It is not transmitted to or stored on any server we operate.

2.2 On-Device Data

  • Favorites: Saved restaurants and personal notes are stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Preferences: Settings such as search radius, sort order, and appearance theme are stored locally on your device.
  • Search Cache: Recent search results are cached on your device for up to 5 minutes to reduce redundant API calls. You can clear this cache at any time from Settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide restaurant search results based on your location
  • Display distances, ratings, hours, and other restaurant details
  • Store your favorites and preferences locally for a personalized experience

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

  • All data is stored on your device. There is no backend server or cloud database.
  • Favorites and preferences persist on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Search cache expires automatically after 5 minutes or can be cleared manually.
  • Photo cache is stored on your device with a 7-day expiry to improve performance.

4.2 Security Measures

  • All network communication uses HTTPS encryption
  • The Google Places API key is restricted to the app's bundle ID, preventing unauthorized use
  • No sensitive data is stored on remote servers

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Places API

  • The app sends your search location directly to Google Places API to retrieve restaurant data
  • Google may process this data according to their own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy

5.2 Apple MapKit

  • We use Apple MapKit for map display and navigation
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to MapKit services: Apple Privacy Policy

5.3 Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

  • Address and ZIP code searches use the Nominatim geocoding service to convert locations to coordinates
  • OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies: OSM Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data: Persists until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Search cache: Automatically cleared after 5 minutes
  • Photo cache: Automatically cleared after 7 days
  • No server-side retention: We do not operate any servers that store your data

7. Your Rights

  • Control: You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings
  • Deletion: Clear your search cache from the app's Settings screen, delete individual favorites, or uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Transparency: No data is collected by us — only the third-party services listed above receive data as part of normal app operation

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: khakichimp@gmail.com

11. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Consent: You grant location permission through iOS before any location data is accessed
  • Legitimate Interest: Providing restaurant search results using the location you provide

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information. Since the app operates entirely on-device with no user accounts, no personal information is collected or stored by us.
