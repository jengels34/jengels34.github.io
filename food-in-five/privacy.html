1Introduction

Food in Five ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application and this website. Food in Five has no user accounts and no backend that stores your data. It collects nothing on our own servers except feedback you choose to send from the app (Section 2.5).

2Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

  • Current Location: With your permission, and only while you're using the app, Food in Five reads your current location to find nearby restaurants and show distances.
  • Where it goes: The search location is sent directly from your device to the Google Places API to retrieve restaurants (Section 5.1). It is never sent to or stored on any server we operate, and it is never included in feedback.
  • Search Locations: You can search around another place instead. A place name you pick from suggestions is looked up with Google Places. An address or ZIP code you type is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service to convert it into coordinates (Section 5.3).
  • You can deny or turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time and still search by address or ZIP code.

2.2 Searches and Photos

  • When you search for a craving, the text you type is sent to Google Places with your search location to find matching restaurants.
  • When you open a restaurant, its Google place ID is sent to Google Places to load hours, phone number and other details.
  • Restaurant photos are downloaded from Google's servers the first time they are shown, then saved on your device. As a result, Google may receive your device's IP address and which photos are being downloaded.
  • We do not store or log your searches or photo requests ourselves.

2.3 On-Device Data

  • Favorites: Saved restaurants, your personal notes and collections are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. They are not synced to iCloud or anywhere else.
  • Preferences: Settings such as default search radius, number of results, sort order, appearance and haptics are stored locally on your device.
  • Search Cache: Recent search results are kept in memory for up to 5 minutes to avoid repeat requests.
  • Photo Cache: Restaurant photos are saved on your device for up to 7 days.
  • Sent Feedback: For each feedback submission, your device keeps its title, type and a random status token (Section 2.5).

2.4 Device Information and On-Device Metrics

  • App version, iOS version and device model, read on your device for local app operation and included with feedback you send
  • Launch, hang and crash figures that Apple's MetricKit gives the app about this device. They are kept on your device and leave it only if you include diagnostics with feedback (Section 2.5).

2.5 Feedback You SendNew

Food in Five sends feedback only when you tap Send in Settings > Send Feedback. Nothing is sent otherwise. We don't ask for your email address. Each submission includes:

  • The feedback type (Bug, Idea or Other), title and details you enter
  • App version and build, iOS version and device model
  • A diagnostics report, only if you turn on its toggle: your locale, location permission status, the last search error, how many favorites you've saved, the launch, hang and crash figures in Section 2.4, and your current app settings (search radius, open-only, price filter, maximum results, sort order, theme and haptics)

Feedback never includes your location coordinates, your contacts, your email address, or the names or notes of your favorites.

Your feedback goes to a service we operate on Cloudflare Workers, which files it as an issue in a private GitHub repository for triage. The service keeps a small record of each submission: the issue number, the title, its status and timestamps, and a SHA-256 hash of the random status token your device keeps. It uses your IP address only to limit how often submissions can be sent, and does not store it.

2.6 Home Screen Widgets and Quick Actions

  • Food in Five's widgets and Home Screen quick actions are shortcuts that open the app. They make no network requests, don't use your location, and hold no personal data.

3How We Use Your Information

  • Your location and searches are sent to Google Places and Nominatim solely to find restaurants near the place you chose
  • Distances, ratings, hours and other restaurant details are shown and sorted on your device
  • Favorites and preferences are stored on your device to give you a personal experience
  • Feedback you send is used to triage and fix problems and to plan improvements
  • We do not use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to track you across apps or websites
  • Apart from feedback you choose to send, we do not collect, transmit, or store your data on our own servers

4Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Favorites, notes, collections and preferences are stored on your device until you delete them or delete the app
  • Caches: Search results expire after 5 minutes and photos after 7 days. Both can be cleared any time from Settings.
  • Feedback: Feedback you send is processed by a Cloudflare Worker we operate and becomes an issue in a private GitHub repository for triage
  • No Other Developer Servers: We do not operate any servers that store your location, searches or favorites

4.2 Security Measures

  • All network requests (to Google Places, Nominatim and our feedback service) are made over HTTPS/TLS
  • The Google Places API key is restricted to the app's bundle ID, so it can't be used outside Food in Five
  • On-device data benefits from iOS sandboxing and device encryption

4.3 No Tracking or Advertising

  • Food in Five contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Food in Five does not track you as Apple defines it, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • This website uses no analytics or advertising of its own

5Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Places API

  • Provides restaurant search, restaurant details, place suggestions and photos
  • Receives the search location and radius, the text you search for, the restaurants you open, photo requests and your IP address
  • Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by its services: Google Privacy Policy

5.2 Apple MapKit

  • Used to show the map. Tapping Directions opens Apple Maps.
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to MapKit and Apple Maps: Apple Privacy Policy

5.3 Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

  • Converts an address or ZIP code you type into coordinates. Only that text is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies: OSM Privacy Policy

5.4 Feedback Processors

  • Cloudflare runs the service that receives feedback
  • GitHub hosts the private repository where feedback is triaged
  • These services process feedback only on our behalf, and their privacy policies apply

5.5 This Website

  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Calling a restaurant, opening its website or sharing it hands off to the Phone app, your browser or the share sheet you choose. What happens there is governed by those apps.

6Data Retention

  • On-Device Data: Favorites and preferences stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app
  • Search Cache: Cleared automatically after 5 minutes
  • Photo Cache: Cleared automatically after 7 days
  • Feedback: Feedback issues are kept as part of our development backlog, together with the small record described in Section 2.5
  • Status Token: Your device keeps a random token for each submission. Our service stores only a hash of it, and no account is involved.
  • Apart from feedback, we hold no server-side data

7Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • Location: Revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings
  • Delete Local Data: Clear the search and photo cache in the app's Settings, delete individual favorites, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Delete Feedback: To have a feedback submission deleted, email us at the address in Section 10 with the feedback's title
  • No Account to Delete: Food in Five does not use accounts, so there is no account deletion process

8Children's Privacy

Food in Five is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Apart from feedback a user chooses to send, the app does not collect any personal data on our servers.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Consent: You grant location permission through iOS before any location data is accessed
  • Legitimate Interest: Providing restaurant search results using the location you provide
  • Consent: For feedback, which is sent only when you tap Send in the app, with diagnostics only if you turn them on

Apart from feedback you choose to send, all data is stored locally on your device and is under your direct control at all times.

12California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You can request details about what personal information we collect. The only personal information we collect on our servers is feedback you choose to send, described in Section 2.5.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete all your data by deleting the app, and request deletion of feedback you sent, as described in Section 7
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell or share personal information
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights
