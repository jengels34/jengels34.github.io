About the app

Find great food fast

Discover great food near you in seconds! Stop scrolling through endless restaurant options and eliminate decision fatigue. Food in Five delivers lightning-fast search results within an adjustable radius of 1 to 15 miles, so you can spend less time searching and more time enjoying great food. All data stays on your device — no account required.

Key features

Everything you need to decide fast

Smart Filters

Filter by Open Now, price level, and adjustable search radius (1–15 miles).

Favorites with Notes

Save your favorite restaurants and add personal notes to remember why you loved them.

Sorting Options

Sort results by Top Rated, price, or alphabetically.

Swipeable Card Carousel

Browse restaurants by swiping through cards synced to the interactive map.

Fully On-Device

No backend server — all data stays on your device for maximum privacy.

Dark Mode

Choose between System, Light, or Dark appearance.

Your privacy matters

On-device by design

No Account Required

Start using the app immediately — no sign-up needed.

On-Device Only

All your data — favorites, preferences, and search cache — stays on your device. There is no backend server.

Location Privacy

Your location is sent directly to Google Places API to find nearby restaurants. It is never stored on any server we operate.

No Data Sharing

We never sell or share your information with third parties.
