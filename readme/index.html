About the app

Track your reading journey

ReadMe is a beautiful book tracking application designed to help you manage your reading journey. Track your reading progress, organise your library, build a wish list, and discover new books — all stored privately on your device with optional iCloud sync.

Key features

What makes it the perfect reading companion

Library Management

Organise your books by status — reading, completed, to-read, or wish list.

Progress Tracking

Log reading sessions with pages and time, and watch your progress grow.

Visual Statistics

Beautiful charts showing your reading habits over time.

Book Search

Find books instantly with Google Books integration, or add them manually.

iCloud Sync

Optionally sync your library across all your Apple devices via your own iCloud account.

Your privacy matters

Your data belongs to you

No Account Required

ReadMe works without any sign-in or account creation — your data belongs to you.

Stored On Your Device

All your reading data is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Your iCloud, Your Data

Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple iCloud account — your data never passes through our servers.

You Control Your Data

Export your library at any time, or delete everything by removing the app and clearing iCloud data.
