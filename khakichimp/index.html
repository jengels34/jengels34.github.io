Built the Apple way

All three apps are written in Swift with SwiftUI, and keep your data on your iPhone with SwiftData. ReadMe and Tasks - Uniflow can sync through iCloud if you turn it on. Each app has Home Screen widgets and uses Apple's own tools where it can: MapKit in Food in Five, Apple Reminders in Tasks - Uniflow, and the camera barcode scanner in ReadMe. No cross-platform shortcuts.

🔒

Privacy isn't a feature - it's the default

No analytics trackers, no ad networks, no hidden data collection. Your data lives on your device (and your iCloud, if you choose). That's the whole point.

🛠️

Built and shipped solo

I design, build, test, and release each app myself, using AI coding tools to move faster. That covers everything from the first idea to App Store review, privacy policies, in-app feedback, and crash reporting. All three are still being updated: ReadMe is on version 3 and Food in Five on version 2.
