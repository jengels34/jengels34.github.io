Built the Apple way

Every Khakichimp app is fully native and declarative — SwiftUI front to back, with data that stays on your device unless you explicitly sync it. No cross-platform shortcuts, no compromise on feel.

🔒

Privacy isn't a feature — it's the default

No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no hidden data collection. Your data lives on your device (and your iCloud, if you choose). That's the whole point.
